## What's new in 0.2.4

### New
- **Focus mode**: shows only your page, full screen, with nothing else on screen. Turn it on with the button in the top bar or **Ctrl+Shift+F**. The toolbar stays hidden until you move the mouse over it. Press **Escape** or **Ctrl+Shift+F** again to leave.

### Fixed
- Pasting a YouTube link now shows a thumbnail card even for videos whose owner turned off embedding. Before, those links stayed plain links.
