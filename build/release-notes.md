## What's new in 0.2.2

### New
- **Toggle lists** — a collapsible block with a title. Type `/` and pick **Toggle list**. Each toggle remembers whether it was open or closed. In exports, toggles are expanded, Markdown keeps them as collapsible blocks and Word shows the title in bold.
- **7 new fonts**: Caveat, Dancing Script, Playfair Display, Space Grotesk, Special Elite, Reem Kufi and Amiri. They're bundled with the app, so they work offline.
- **Lighter color shades**: each font color now has two lighter shades. Pink was removed, and the color swatches are a bit smaller.

### Improved
- The comment icon now sits right after the commented text as a bright amber badge that's easy to see in light and dark mode. The note area no longer needs a side margin for it.
- **Sections column**: drag it past its minimum width to collapse it, and drag it back to reopen it. When it reopens, it resizes to fit your section titles.
- The Sections and Pages sidebar can't be wider than 29% of the window.
- Graph view nodes are shaded to look 3D instead of flat circles.
- The table of contents has a close button.
- The text style dropdown is now labeled **Turn into**.
- Copied text keeps single line breaks, so it no longer pastes double-spaced.
- If the Sections column is collapsed, the Tags view tells you to expand it to see your tags.

### Fixed
- The save indicator no longer gets stuck on "Saving..." when the app reloads.
- Colored heading rows no longer cause a horizontal scrollbar.
- The graph view settings button now closes its own popover.
- The top of the Settings panel now lines up with the top of the note.
- The status bar divider is thicker and matches the toolbar dividers.
