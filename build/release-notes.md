## What's new in 0.2.3

### Improved
- **Keyboard shortcuts** now open in their own popup from Settings, instead of a long list inside the panel.
- The **Properties** panel remembers whether you collapsed or expanded it, across all pages and restarts. Its rows are also indented to line up with the title.
- The font-family toolbar button now shows an italic **f**, so it's easier to tell apart from the other buttons.
- The **About** section in Settings is shorter, with the app name in the heading.
- The right-click **Comment** item no longer shows a shortcut hint.

### Fixed
- Clicking a heading in the table of contents now scrolls to it.
- Pressing F11 after clicking the Properties toggle no longer leaves an outline around it.
