## What's new in 0.2.5

### New
- **Add rows and columns right on the table**: hover a table (or put your cursor in it) and a **+** bar appears on its right and bottom edges. Click it to add a column or a row. The toolbar's add row/column buttons are gone.
- `Ctrl+\` opens and closes the sidebar. It's listed in Keyboard shortcuts.

### Improved
- The sidebar's Open/Close button and the collapsed Sections/Tags tab now show the same tooltips as the toolbar.
- The collapsed Sections/Tags tab lines up with the sidebar toggle.

### Fixed
- Pressing F11 after clicking a section or page no longer leaves an outline around it.
